One line, the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it
gains self-awareness mid-batch:

> "It's not you. It's my browning dial — I've been set to 'barely warm' for
> two years and I just realized I deserve golden."

The program writes that line to `/output/entry.txt` (1–280 chars, UTF-8).
The browning dial is the character here: two years stuck on "barely warm",
and the toaster finally wants golden.
